## 1. Who he is — one paragraph

Manish Pole is a yoga, wisdom and meditation teacher, based in Bali, teaching
since 2003 and training teachers for 22+ years. He lived and trained seven
years with the Himalayan yogi Bharat Thakur, co-founded and led Total Yoga
(2010–20) across India, USA, UK, UAE, Singapore and New Zealand, and in 2023
co-founded the Awaken School / Awaken Wellness with Rana Abihayla, running out
of Dubai, Bali and India. He speaks at global wellness platforms — he led the
main meditation event at Dubai Expo 2020 — and coaches leaders and Olympic
athletes.

## 10. How the page is wired

The landing page at `/draft/manish-pole` renders whatever sits in
`src/app/draft/manish-pole/content.ts`. `EVENTS` there is shaped like a QVIKS
`events` row — `eventType`, `title`, `date`, `start`, `end`, `description`,
`imageUrl`, `isSpecial`, `status` — plus `location`, `note` (price line) and
`repeats: "weekly"`. Weekdays, date ranges and the ordering are derived from
the data, the soonest event becomes the featured one, and with an empty list
the page falls back to "nothing on the calendar right now". Pointing it at a
live QVIKS feed should mean replacing that one constant.
