# Outreach strategy

## Niche (decided 2026-09-09)

Retreat organizers/creators. Europe. English-speaking market.

**Why this is a strong niche, not just "a" niche:** retreats need a landing page
(mikistec) *and* structurally need booking + deposit/payment handling + participant
CRM (qviks) — tighter product-market fit than generic small business, and both
funnel stages sell to the same specific pain, not a vague "grow your business."
Seasonal/repeat retreats also mean repeat landing-page work, not just one-off.

**Where to source them:** Instagram (very visual niche, retreat accounts/hashtags),
retreat marketplaces (BookRetreats, Retreat Guru, Tripaneer — organizer contact info
often findable from listings), yoga/wellness alliance directories, wellness Facebook
groups.
