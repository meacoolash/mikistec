# stec.sk overview

Personal CV/portfolio for Mikuláš Štec, live at **www.stec.sk**, deployed on Vercel.

Two related local repos:
- `../project-resume` — the live site (`git log`/`.git` present, actively maintained,
  most recent). Next.js + Tailwind, pulls CV content from `Data.tsx`, theme switcher,
  print/PDF export, Formspree email integration.
- `../project-resume-public` — appears to be a public/sanitized fork or mirror of the
  same codebase (near-identical file list, separate git history). Purpose relative to
  `project-resume` isn't confirmed — worth a one-line check with Miki if it becomes
  relevant (e.g. is it a public template version meant for others to reuse, or just a
  stale duplicate).

## Role in the business

**Corrected 2026-09-09** — not a revenue asset for mikistec. stec.sk is Miki's
corporate-job-search CV: the audience is hiring managers/employers evaluating him for
a full-time software development role, not small business owners buying a website.

**Decision: keep it separate, don't link it from mikistec.** A prospect evaluating
"should I hire this person's agency to run my website" is undermined, not reassured,
by discovering the same person is also job-hunting for full-time employment — it
raises "are they even going to stick around" doubts that don't serve the sale. The
two sites target different buyers with different, mildly conflicting signals
(available-for-hire employee vs. running an ongoing service business). No link
either direction unless that changes.
